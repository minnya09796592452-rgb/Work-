# Asset and IP Policy

## Permitted
- Content created specifically for CASTAWAY DEPTHS.
- Third-party content whose license clearly permits intended use and redistribution.
- Public-domain content with verified provenance.

## Prohibited by default
Do not include Stranded Deep code, DLLs, executables, extracted textures, meshes, audio, maps, UI, logos, story text, names, or other protected expressive content. Do not use unclear-license material.

## Reference boundary
Study broad genre conventions and abstract gameplay ideas only. Independently design distinctive characters, locations, items, systems, UI, narrative, and visual expression.

## Required provenance
For every imported asset record asset ID/path, creator/source, license/version, license URL or proof, modifications, date added, and SHA-256. Unknown provenance means the asset is excluded from distributable builds.
